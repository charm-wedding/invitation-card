01 / A story called us

Two hearts. Two worlds.
One beautiful story.

— ✦ —

Somewhere between Param’s voyages and Charu’s world of code, two very different worlds found something wonderfully common — each other.

Param

A Punjabi munda & a seafarer

He loves exploring the world, discovering good food, watching movies, and never saying no to a game of cricket. ⚓ 🍿 🏏

Charu

Grace, ambition & warmth

A software developer who loves dancing, music, little celebrations, and naturally, a good plate of golgappas. Her smile lights up even the simplest moments. 💃 ✨

Param di duniya te Charu di muskaan became a story of their own. Through heartfelt conversations, quiet understanding, and a thousand treasured memories, what began as “just getting to know each other” slowly unfolded into something tender, steady, and true.

With every passing moment, their hearts found their way closer, until love became the compass guiding them here. Together, they’re ready to sail into their greatest adventure yet — a lifetime woven with love, laughter, and dreams shared hand in hand.

Two cultures. Two personalities. One beautiful love story.

With their families by their side, hearts full of love, and a little Punjabi masti along the way, they invite you to celebrate the beginning of Param & Charu’s forever. ❤️

With love, Charu & Param
